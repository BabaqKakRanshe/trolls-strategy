Indie Meditations (Minimalist & Cozy Vibes) FREE Music Pack

OVERVIEW
This free game music pack was created by YannZ (Yanni Ziangos).
"Minimalist & Cozy Vibes" includes 10 seamless loop tracks designed to enhance peaceful, introspective, and exploration-heavy moments in your indie game. Perfect for menu ambience, storytelling, and soft-level progression.

Each track is composed in 120 BPM and in F (major, minor, or modal), making them easy to blend and align with real-time or timed game elements.

USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

Copy the Audio/ folder into your game project.

For prototyping or browser demos, use the MP3/ or OGG/ formats.

For final builds or trailer mixes, use the WAV/ versions.

All tracks loop cleanly and may be edited to suit your scene.

Maintain file naming and metadata for attribution and ease of use.
